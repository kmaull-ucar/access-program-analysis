
# Altmetrics Impact

The publications represented in this Altmetrics list
are those that have **between 5 and 15 citations**, and
an Altmetric score **greater than 50**.  You 
can find more details about the components
of the score [here](https://help.altmetric.com/support/solutions/articles/6000234288-altmetric-attention-score-modifiers).

**A score above 50** is typically considered among the **top 5%** of Altmetric scores. 

Altmetrics scores are generally used as a metric for 
understanding _potential_ impact of a publication
and it **does not necessarily** correlate to citation counts or actual impact.  It can be used in
conjuction with other metrics to paint a more complete picture of 
a publication's impact.
